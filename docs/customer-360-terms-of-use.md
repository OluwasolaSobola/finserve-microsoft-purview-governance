# Customer 360 Banking Data – Terms of Use

## Purpose

These Terms of Use define the governance requirements for accessing and using the FinServe Bank Customer 360 Banking Data product.

The data product brings together governed customer, customer-master, CRM and account data to support authorised analytical and operational use cases.

## Permitted Use

The Customer 360 Banking Data product may be used for authorised purposes including:

- Customer identification and Customer 360 analysis
- Customer-account relationship analysis
- Customer master data reconciliation
- Data quality monitoring and remediation
- Duplicate and identity-resolution analysis
- Governed business analytics
- Approved regulatory or operational analysis

Use of the data must be consistent with the approved business purpose stated in the access request.

## Access Requirements

Access must:

- Be requested for a legitimate business purpose.
- Include sufficient business justification.
- Be approved through the applicable data access process.
- Be limited to the period required for the approved purpose.
- Follow applicable role-based access controls and least-privilege principles.

Access does not automatically authorise onward sharing or unrestricted reuse of the data.

## Data Protection and Confidentiality

The data product contains customer-related and potentially sensitive business information.

Users must:

- Protect personal and confidential information from unauthorised access or disclosure.
- Use the data only for the approved purpose.
- Follow applicable privacy, security and data governance requirements.
- Avoid exposing customer information through unauthorised reports, extracts or applications.
- Report suspected misuse, unauthorised access or data incidents through the appropriate governance or security process.

## Data Copies and Distribution

Uncontrolled copies of the Customer 360 Banking Data product are not permitted.

Users must not:

- Create unnecessary local copies.
- Distribute extracts to unauthorised users.
- Upload governed data to unapproved systems or services.
- Circumvent approved access controls.

Where an extract is legitimately required, it must remain subject to appropriate access, security, retention and disposal controls.

## Data Quality

Data consumers must consider the documented data quality status of the assets before using them for analysis or decision-making.

A high technical data quality score does not guarantee that all potential business-quality issues are absent.

Known findings, exceptions and limitations should be reviewed where relevant to the intended use.

Suspected data quality issues should be raised for assessment and remediation rather than corrected through uncontrolled local changes.

## Data Stewardship

Data owners and stewards are responsible for supporting the appropriate interpretation, quality and governance of data within their areas of responsibility.

Data consumers should escalate:

- Suspected data quality defects
- Incorrect or ambiguous metadata
- Classification concerns
- Access-control issues
- Potential privacy or security incidents

to the appropriate data owner, steward or governance function.

## Retention and Disposal

Data obtained through authorised access must not be retained longer than required for the approved purpose.

Temporary extracts and derived copies must be securely disposed of when they are no longer required, subject to applicable retention requirements.

## Compliance

Use of the Customer 360 Banking Data product is subject to applicable:

- Data governance policies and standards
- Information security requirements
- Privacy and data protection requirements
- Data retention requirements
- Access-control requirements
- Regulatory obligations

Failure to comply with these requirements may result in access being withdrawn.

## Acceptance

By requesting or using access to the Customer 360 Banking Data product, the user acknowledges these Terms of Use and agrees to use the data only for authorised purposes and in accordance with applicable governance controls.
